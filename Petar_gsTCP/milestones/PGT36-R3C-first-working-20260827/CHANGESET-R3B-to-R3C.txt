One executable behavior correction at PumpYieldNetwork:

R3B accidentally restored the pre-HARDEN FULL-PADDLE bypass:
    lda PumpRescueActive
    beq M3R21FNormalControl
    brl M3R21FFullPaddleBoundary

That skipped CheckNetworkControlKey while Always-Paddle was active,
functionally locking out ESC/C/R/D even though the latch routines were
still present in the binary.

R3C restores the HARDEN-R1K1 safe boundary verbatim:
    jsr ReadRingCounters
    jsr UpdateProducerLead
    jsr CheckNetworkControlKey
